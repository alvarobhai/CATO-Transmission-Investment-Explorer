Methodology & Sources

Purpose

This dashboard is an opportunity explorer, not an Ofgem eligibility engine. It identifies transmission projects exhibiting characteristics that may be relevant to competitive procurement and lets users test different definitions of the potential opportunity universe.

CATO-screened ≠ CATO eligible.
The TWR CATO fields are retained as screening evidence from the underlying master dataset. They should not be interpreted as an Ofgem determination.

Default universe

The default view contains Beyond 2030 – Selected and TWR. Beyond 2030 – Additional is available through the additional filters / expanded universe option.

Filtering

Primary controls cover project type, indicative size, delivery window, new/existing characteristics, separability and investment driver. Advanced source and planning-stage filters are available separately.

Important data limitations

The master dataset currently contains 123 records: 43 Beyond 2030 – Selected, 21 TWR and 59 Beyond 2030 – Additional. MW/capacity is populated for the TWR packages but not generally for the Beyond 2030 records. Some project types and screening fields are therefore classified analytically for dashboard use.

Source methodology

The master dataset defines Beyond 2030 – Selected as options currently recommended as Proceed or Hold; TWR as connection-driven transmission investment packages derived from the TWR CATO screening; and Beyond 2030 – Additional as the wider strategic option universe outside the current Selected bucket.
